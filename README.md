📌 Project Overview
This dashboard analyzes 1,200+ sales orders to identify top-performing regions, products, and payment modes. Built entirely in Advanced Excel using real-world sales data.

Dataset: Sales_Raw_Data.csv (1,202 Orders, 2024)
Tools Used: Advanced Excel, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, TRIM/PROPER, SUMIFS

🔍 Key Business Insights
South region gives 29.1% of total sales (₹45.9 Cr) - Highest performing region, focus marketing budget here.
UPI payment is most preferred with 31.5% share - Optimize payment offers to increase conversion.
Top category is Electronics - Dominates portfolio, expand product line in this segment.
Monthly trend peaks in Dec - Plan inventory before peak season to avoid stock-out.
🛠️ What I Did (For Resume)
Cleaned 1,200+ rows of sales data using TRIM, PROPER, Remove Duplicates & Date transformation
Built 5 Pivot Tables & 5 Pivot Charts to analyze sales by Region, Month, Category & Payment Mode
Designed interactive dashboard with 4 KPI cards, Slicers for dynamic filtering
Identified South region as top performer (29.1% revenue, ₹45.9 Cr) and UPI as preferred payment (31.5%)
